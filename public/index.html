Fake Artist

Everyone adds to one drawing.
One of you has no idea what it is.

Players on this device

Room code
Brush

How to play

Everyone builds one drawing together. All but one player know the secret word. The fake artist only knows the category and has to bluff.

Each turn

  • Players take turns adding to the shared canvas. Draw enough to show you know the word, but not so much that the fake artist can work it out.
  • Ink budget mode: each turn you get a limited amount of ink and can draw several strokes until it runs out.
  • Free whiteboard mode: draw as much as you like on your turn, then tap End turn.
  • One stroke each mode: one stroke per turn, any length.
  • Rounds: the host can set a fixed number of rounds, or pick "Until players vote to end". Then everyone votes after each round on whether the drawing is done, and a majority of yes ends it. During a round, tap Finish drawing to vote to stop early. If a majority of players do, it goes straight to the vote on who the fake is. Tap again to take your vote back.

Vote

When drawing ends, everyone secretly votes for who they think the fake artist is. A tie lets the fake artist escape.

Winning

  • If the fake artist is not caught, they win.
  • If they are caught, they get one chance to guess the word. A right guess still wins it for them.
  • Otherwise the real artists win.

One device

Each player taps their own card to see their role and word, and taps it again to hide it. Secret votes are passed around the device one player at a time.
